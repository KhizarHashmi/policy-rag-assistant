# General Terms and Conditions

These terms apply to every Lumen Health Assurance hospital and surgical plan (Essential, Plus and Premier).
Plan-specific limits are set out in each plan's Schedule of Benefits. Where this document and a Schedule
of Benefits differ, the Schedule of Benefits prevails.

## Definitions

**Eligible expenses** means reasonable and customary charges for medically necessary treatment that is
covered by your plan, up to the limits in your Schedule of Benefits.

**Deductible** means the amount of eligible expenses you pay each policy year before the plan starts paying.

**Co-insurance** means the percentage of eligible expenses, after the deductible, that you continue to pay.

**Pre-existing condition** means any illness, injury or symptom for which you received treatment, medication
or advice, or of which you were aware, in the 5 years before your cover started.

**In-network hospital** means a hospital that has a direct-billing agreement with us. The current list is
published in the member portal.

## Waiting Periods

A general waiting period of 30 days applies from the cover start date to all illnesses, except accidental
injuries, which are covered immediately. Pre-existing conditions and maternity benefits have additional
waiting periods stated in each Schedule of Benefits.

## General Exclusions

We will not pay for:

1. Cosmetic or aesthetic surgery and treatment, unless needed to restore function after an accident covered by the policy.
2. Experimental or unproven treatment, or drugs not approved by the national health regulator.
3. Self-inflicted injury, or injury caused by the abuse of alcohol or drugs.
4. Dental treatment, unless needed because of an accidental injury to sound natural teeth and given within 30 days of the accident.
5. Fertility treatment, assisted conception and contraception.
6. Weight-loss (bariatric) surgery, unless your body mass index is 40 or more and you have a related medical condition such as type 2 diabetes, and the treatment is pre-authorised.
7. Treatment in the United States of America, except where your plan's emergency overseas benefit explicitly includes it.
8. Expenses arising from war, nuclear contamination, or taking part in professional sports.

## Making a Claim

For treatment at an in-network hospital, the hospital bills us directly; you only pay the deductible,
co-insurance and any non-covered items.

For all other treatment, submit your claim through the member portal within 90 days of the date of discharge
(or of the outpatient treatment). Claims submitted after 90 days are not payable, unless you can show it was
not reasonably possible to submit them earlier.

Every claim must include:

- the original itemised invoice showing each charge;
- the discharge summary or medical report stating the diagnosis;
- proof of payment, if you paid the provider yourself;
- the pre-authorisation reference, if pre-authorisation was required.

We aim to settle complete claims within 10 working days.

## Currency of Payment

Claims are paid in the currency of your policy. Expenses incurred in another currency are converted using the
exchange rate published by the Monetary Authority of Singapore on the date of treatment.

## Fraud

If any claim is fraudulent or intentionally exaggerated, we will not pay it, we may cancel your policy from the
date of the fraudulent act, and we may recover any amounts already paid.
