# Essential Plan - Schedule of Benefits

All amounts are in SGD and apply per insured person per policy year unless stated otherwise.

## Overall Annual Limit

The maximum we pay for all benefits combined is SGD 150,000 per policy year.

## Deductible and Co-insurance

A deductible of SGD 3,500 applies each policy year. After the deductible, you pay co-insurance of 10% of
eligible expenses. Co-insurance is capped at SGD 3,000 per policy year.

## Hospital Room and Board

Standard ward (4-bed) accommodation is covered up to SGD 250 per day. If you choose a higher room class,
we pay the Standard-ward limit and you pay the difference.

Intensive care unit (ICU) charges are covered up to SGD 1,000 per day.

## Surgical and Medical Fees

Surgeon, anaesthetist and specialist fees are covered according to the Ministry of Health fee benchmarks.
Charges above the benchmark are not covered.

## Outpatient Treatment

Outpatient treatment is not covered, except follow-up treatment for the same condition within 90 days after
a covered hospital admission.

## Cancer Treatment

Chemotherapy, radiotherapy and targeted therapy are covered up to SGD 30,000 per policy year.
Biologic and immunotherapy drugs are covered only if they are on the national Cancer Drug List.

## Maternity

Maternity, pregnancy and childbirth are not covered under the Essential plan.

## Emergency Treatment Overseas

Treatment received outside Singapore is not covered under the Essential plan.

## Pre-existing Conditions

Pre-existing conditions are covered only after a waiting period of 24 months of continuous cover, and only if
they were declared on the application form.
