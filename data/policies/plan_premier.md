# Premier Plan - Schedule of Benefits

All amounts are in SGD and apply per insured person per policy year unless stated otherwise.

## Overall Annual Limit

The maximum we pay for all benefits combined is SGD 2,000,000 per policy year.

## Deductible and Co-insurance

There is no deductible on the Premier plan. Treatment at in-network hospitals has no co-insurance.
Treatment at out-of-network hospitals carries co-insurance of 20% of eligible expenses, uncapped.

## Hospital Room and Board

Private room accommodation of any class is covered as charged.

Intensive care unit (ICU) charges are covered as charged.

## Surgical and Medical Fees

Surgeon, anaesthetist and specialist fees are covered as charged, provided they are reasonable and customary.

## Outpatient Treatment

Specialist and general practitioner consultations, diagnostic tests, prescribed medicines and physiotherapy
are covered up to SGD 10,000 per policy year. An annual health screening is covered up to SGD 500.

## Cancer Treatment

All cancer treatment, including biologic and immunotherapy drugs, is covered as charged, with no annual
sub-limit. Pre-authorisation is required.

## Mental Health

Inpatient psychiatric treatment is covered for up to 30 days per policy year.

## Maternity

Maternity benefits are available after a waiting period of 10 months from the cover start date.
We pay up to SGD 8,000 for a normal delivery and up to SGD 12,000 for a caesarean delivery. Complications
of pregnancy are covered as charged.

## Emergency Treatment Overseas

Emergency treatment anywhere in the world, including the United States of America, is covered up to
SGD 500,000 per policy year.

## Pre-existing Conditions

Declared pre-existing conditions are covered after a waiting period of 12 months of continuous cover.
