# Plus Plan - Schedule of Benefits

All amounts are in SGD and apply per insured person per policy year unless stated otherwise.

## Overall Annual Limit

The maximum we pay for all benefits combined is SGD 500,000 per policy year.

## Deductible and Co-insurance

A deductible of SGD 1,500 applies each policy year. After the deductible, you pay co-insurance of 10% of
eligible expenses. Co-insurance is capped at SGD 2,000 per policy year.

## Hospital Room and Board

Single-bed private room accommodation is covered up to SGD 450 per day.

Intensive care unit (ICU) charges are covered up to SGD 1,800 per day.

## Surgical and Medical Fees

Surgeon, anaesthetist and specialist fees are covered as charged, provided they are reasonable and customary.

## Outpatient Treatment

Specialist outpatient consultations, diagnostic tests and prescribed medicines are covered up to SGD 3,000 per
policy year. Physiotherapy is covered for up to 12 sessions per policy year when referred by a specialist.

## Cancer Treatment

Chemotherapy, radiotherapy, targeted therapy and biologic or immunotherapy drugs are covered up to
SGD 120,000 per policy year.

## Maternity

Maternity benefits are available after a waiting period of 10 months from the cover start date.
We pay up to SGD 4,000 for a normal delivery and up to SGD 6,000 for a caesarean delivery, including
pre- and post-natal care. Complications of pregnancy are covered up to SGD 15,000.

## Emergency Treatment Overseas

Emergency treatment needed while travelling in Asia for up to 90 days per trip is covered up to SGD 50,000
per policy year. Planned treatment overseas is not covered.

## Pre-existing Conditions

Pre-existing conditions are covered after a waiting period of 12 months of continuous cover, if declared on the
application form.
